# Noumee Pricing Specs

Final pricing and plan specifications for the Noumee website prototype.

This file is the current pricing source of truth for the website.

## Billing

- Default website state: Yearly
- Billing toggle options: Monthly and Yearly
- Yearly value message: Save up to 32%
- Yearly card display should not mention the average monthly equivalent; keep equivalents only in this spec table.

## Shared Paid Plan Notes

All paid plans include:

- PWA install
- vCard download
- NFC tap sharing

## Plan Details

### Free

Included:

- 1 profile
- Dynamic QR

Not included / limited:

- Limited editing

### One

Included:

- 1 profile
- Dynamic QR
- Free profile editing
- Template choice
- Lead capture
- AI Avatar: 1 generation/month, Pixar style

Card:

- NFC card: EUR 19 one-time
- Noumee branded

### Plus

Label: Most popular

Included:

- 1 profile
- Dynamic QR
- Free profile editing
- Template choice
- Lead capture
- Advanced analytics
- AI Avatar: 3 generations/month, Pixar style

Card:

- NFC card: EUR 29 one-time
- Customizable metallic physical card

### Elite

Included:

- 3 profiles
- Dynamic QR
- Free profile editing
- Template choice
- Lead capture
- Advanced analytics
- Verified badge
- AI Avatar: 10 generations/month, all styles

Card:

- NFC card: EUR 29 one-time
- Customizable metallic physical card
